# Linux Interview Questions

Every question here links back to the full topic page it's taught on — use this hub to find the
right set for your experience level or target company, then click through to prepare from the
real explanation, not just a memorized answer.

## By difficulty tier

- **[Beginner](beginner.md)** — Linux basics, terminal and shell, files and the filesystem, permissions and users, processes. For 0-2 years of experience.
- **[Intermediate](intermediate.md)** — cron and shell scripting, security, monitoring, performance, containers, Kubernetes, cloud/DevOps, cybersecurity, and web servers. For roughly 2-5 years of experience.
- **[Senior/Expert](senior.md)** — systemd, networking, and SSH internals, LVM/RAID, logging architecture, SELinux policy, kernel tuning, and infrastructure design-from-scratch questions.
- **[Scenario-Based](scenario-based.md)** — incident and ticket-style questions: "you get paged, what do you do," across storage, services, networking, security, and Kubernetes.
