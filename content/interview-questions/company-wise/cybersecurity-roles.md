# Linux Interview Questions — Cybersecurity Roles

> These interview questions are compiled from publicly shared interview experiences, official
> documentation, community discussions, and common industry practices. They are representative
> examples and not confidential or leaked interview material.

Cybersecurity, SOC analyst, and pentest interviews test Linux as an attack surface and a defense
layer — access control models, hardening, and log-based investigation, more than general sysadmin
skills.

- **[What's the difference between DAC and MAC in Linux security?](../intermediate.md#dac-vs-mac)** *(Intermediate)* — foundational access-control theory this field is built on.
- **[What's the difference between SELinux "enforcing" and "permissive" mode?](../intermediate.md#selinux-enforcing-vs-permissive)** *(Intermediate)* — a practical Mandatory Access Control question asked across security roles.
- **[What's the difference between a vulnerability scan and a penetration test?](../intermediate.md#vuln-scan-vs-pentest)** *(Intermediate)* — a fundamental distinction pentest and SOC interviewers check early.
- **[When would you use `nmap` versus `tcpdump`?](../intermediate.md#nmap-vs-tcpdump-use-cases)** *(Intermediate)* — core tool fluency for both offensive and defensive roles.
- **[What Linux skills does a SOC analyst rely on daily that a general sysadmin might not use as often?](../intermediate.md#soc-analyst-linux-skills)** *(Intermediate)* — directly maps to what a SOC analyst role actually does day to day.
- **[A service is being blocked by SELinux in a way no existing policy module covers. How do you resolve it without just disabling SELinux?](../senior.md#selinux-custom-policy-authoring)** *(Senior)* — tests real policy discipline versus the common shortcut of disabling SELinux.
- **[As a defender, what's your systematic approach to finding privilege escalation vectors on a Linux host before an attacker does?](../senior.md#detect-privilege-escalation-vectors)** *(Senior)* — a direct defensive-thinking question central to this field.
- **[How does following a CIS Benchmark differ from general-purpose server hardening?](../senior.md#cis-benchmark-vs-general-hardening)** *(Senior)* — compliance-aware hardening comes up often in enterprise security roles.
- **[You spot repeated failed SSH login attempts from an unfamiliar IP in the auth logs, followed by one successful login. What's your immediate response?](../scenario-based.md#suspicious-login-activity-in-auth-logs)** *(Scenario)* — a realistic incident-response scenario for a SOC or security-focused interview.
