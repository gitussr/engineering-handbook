# Linux Interview Questions — Company-Wise

> These interview questions are compiled from publicly shared interview experiences, official
> documentation, community discussions, and common industry practices. They are representative
> examples and not confidential or leaked interview material.

Questions grouped by company **type** — not by individual named employers — so you can focus on
the flavor of interview you're actually preparing for.

- **[Startup Companies](startup.md)** — generalist questions for engineers who own a server end-to-end.
- **[Product Companies](product-companies.md)** — companies running their own infrastructure at scale.
- **[Service / IT Consulting Companies](service-companies.md)** — broad, fundamentals-first questions across varied client environments.
- **[FAANG-Style Interviews](faang-style.md)** — deep internals and design-from-scratch questions.
- **[Cloud Companies](cloud-companies.md)** — provisioning, IAM, infrastructure as code, container runtimes.
- **[DevOps Roles](devops-roles.md)** — automation, monitoring, containers, and CI/CD incident scenarios.
- **[Linux Administrator Roles](linux-administrator-roles.md)** — filesystem internals, permissions, and disk troubleshooting.
- **[Cybersecurity Roles](cybersecurity-roles.md)** — access control, hardening, vulnerability scanning, and incident response.
